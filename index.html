📋 copy classes from AISIS (bookmark button)

A bookmark you click while you're on AISIS's Schedule of Classes page. It copies every section shown, all pages, already in this site's format.

  1. Add the button once: drag 📋 AISIS → Enlistment Hub to your bookmarks bar. On a phone or if dragging doesn't work, make any bookmark, edit it, and replace its address with the code below.
  2. On AISIS's Schedule of Classes page, choose the term, then click the bookmark. A small panel offers every department (it reads them one by one, about a minute, then you tap copy) or this list only (the department on screen). Pair sections in Remarks fill in Pair Subject.
  3. Paste: into your term tab in the Google Sheet (click the first empty cell in the Subject column, Ctrl+V), or into the paste rows box here if you're not using the sheet.

Different times on different days? Separate both with commas: Monday, Thursday and 0730-0900, 1300-1430.
